## Instructions

1. Combine the olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic, Greek yogurt, and salt.
2. Coat the chicken thoroughly in the marinade. Let it sit while the rest of prep begins; refrigerate if holding for an extended period.
3. Arrange the chicken in a single layer on sheet pans.
4. Bake at 400°F for about 40 minutes, rotating the pans as needed.
5. Cook until the thickest pieces reach at least 165°F. Hold hot for service.

## Notes

* Scaled from a 12-thigh recipe. Assuming roughly 12 boneless thighs is 3 lb, this is about a 13⅓× batch, with the marinade rounded to practical kitchen measurements.
