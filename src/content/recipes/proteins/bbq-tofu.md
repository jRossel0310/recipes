## Instructions

1. Press the tofu at least 30 minutes to remove excess water, then cut into cubes or slabs.
2. **Baked:** Preheat the oven to 450°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Brush the tofu all over with BBQ sauce and arrange in a single layer. Bake 20 minutes until browned around the edges, then flip, brush with more sauce, and bake 5 more minutes until well browned.
3. **Grilled (alternative):** Preheat a grill or grill pan to medium-high. Brush the tofu with BBQ sauce, threading cubes onto skewers if using. Oil the grates well. Grill 2-3 minutes per side until well-charred, brushing with more sauce before serving.
4. Serve with extra BBQ sauce on the side if desired.

## Notes

* A tofu press works best, but wrapping the tofu in kitchen towels weighted down with cans works too.
* Leftovers keep in an airtight container up to 3 days.
* Nutrition is a rough estimate - depends heavily on the BBQ sauce used.
