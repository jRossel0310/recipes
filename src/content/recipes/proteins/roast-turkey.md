## Instructions

1. Thaw all turkeys completely in advance using safe refrigeration procedures.
2. Assign each turkey a roasting pan and oven slot.
3. The night before, cover each turkey with a generous amount of herb butter, both on top of and underneath the skin.
4. Verify the thickest parts of the breast and thigh reach at least 165°F.
5. Allow turkeys to rest before carving.
6. Collect drippings and rendered fat for gravy.
7. Carve and transfer to serving trays.

## Notes

* Equipment: large roasting pans, aluminum foil, meat thermometers, carving knives, and large serving trays.
* Nutrition is a rough estimate (about 6 oz cooked meat per serving) - verify and adjust.
