## Instructions

1. Drain and press or dry the tofu well.
2. Slice the tofu into relatively thin pieces. At this volume, uniform slices matter more than matching the original grater method exactly.
3. Combine the olive oil, lemon juice, maple syrup or agave, spices, garlic, and cornstarch. Add enough water to make the mixture easy to coat with.
4. Toss the tofu gently with the shawarma marinade.
5. Spread across lined sheet pans without piling the tofu too deeply.
6. Bake at 375°F until browned and beginning to crisp at the edges, roughly 20-30 minutes in the convection ovens. Rotate the pans during cooking.

## Notes

* About 45× the original recipe.
* The original recipe uses honey. Maple syrup or agave is substituted here so the tofu stays vegan.
