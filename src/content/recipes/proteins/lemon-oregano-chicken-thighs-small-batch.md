## Instructions

1. Combine the olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic, Greek yogurt, and salt.
2. Coat the chicken thoroughly in the marinade. Let it sit while you prep everything else; refrigerate if holding for more than 30 minutes.
3. Arrange the chicken in a single layer on a sheet pan.
4. Bake at 400°F for about 40 minutes, rotating the pan halfway through.
5. Cook until the thickest pieces reach at least 165°F.
