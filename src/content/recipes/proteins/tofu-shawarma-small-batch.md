## Instructions

1. Drain and press or dry the tofu well.
2. Slice the tofu thinly, or grate it on the coarse side of a box grater.
3. Combine the olive oil, lemon juice, maple syrup or agave, spices, garlic, and cornstarch. Add a splash of water so the mixture is easy to coat with.
4. Toss the tofu gently with the shawarma marinade.
5. Spread across a lined sheet pan in a thin layer.
6. Bake at 375°F until browned and beginning to crisp at the edges, roughly 20-30 minutes. Stir or rotate the pan halfway through.

## Notes

* The original recipe uses honey. Maple syrup or agave keeps the tofu vegan.
* Home-size version of the co-op batch, which is about 45× this amount per 200 g of tofu.
