## Instructions

1. Rinse the chicken in a wash of equal parts cold water and vinegar with the lemon juice added, to clean and tenderize. Pat dry.
2. Combine the salt, black pepper, chili powder, smoked paprika, and garlic powder for the seasoning blend. Season the chicken generously on all sides.
3. Preheat the oven to 400°F and arrange the chicken skin-side up on a rimmed baking sheet.
4. Bake 35-45 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 165°F and the skin is browned.
5. Meanwhile, melt the butter and stir in the hot sauce, granulated garlic, and salt for the buffalo sauce. Keep warm.
6. Toss or brush the baked chicken generously with the buffalo sauce before serving.

## Notes

* Same base chicken and seasoning as the Honey BBQ Chicken recipe, finished with buffalo sauce instead - use one baking sheet of each if cooking side by side.
