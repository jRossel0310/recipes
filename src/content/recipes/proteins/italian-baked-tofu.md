## Instructions

1. Drain the tofu and press it for at least 30 minutes.
2. Slice the block in half through its thickness, then cut each slab into eight squares (16 squares per package).
3. Place the squares in a shallow dish.
4. Stir together the tomato sauce, water, soy sauce, balsamic vinegar, olive oil, garlic, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes.
5. Pour over the tofu and stir gently to coat. Marinate at least 30 minutes.
6. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment and arrange the tofu in an even layer.
7. Bake 40-50 minutes, flipping halfway and spooning any extra marinade over the pieces. It's done when the pieces have darkened and shrunk and the marinade has thickened and mostly dried.
8. Serve right away, or let it cool. It keeps firming up and gets better as it cools.

## Notes

* "Servings" here counts packages: one recipe is one 14 oz block. The source doesn't give a per-person serving count.
* The tofu can marinate for up to 24 hours. Cover and refrigerate it if marinating longer than 2 hours.
