## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F and grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until al dente. Drain well.
3. Melt the butter in a large pot or skillet over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook 1 minute, until lightly browned.
4. Whisk in the evaporated milk, heavy cream, dijon mustard, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, salt, and smoked paprika. Taste and adjust seasoning.
5. Slowly whisk in two-thirds of the cheese until melted and smooth.
6. Stir the hot sauce and melted butter for the buffalo sauce into the cheese sauce until combined. Stir in the drained pasta.
7. Spread half the mixture into the baking dish. Sprinkle with half the remaining cheese.
8. Add the rest of the pasta mixture on top, sprinkle with the remaining cheese, and dust with a little extra smoked paprika.
9. Bake 25 minutes until bubbly and golden.

## Notes

* Same base as the Baked Creamy Mac and Cheese recipe, with buffalo sauce stirred in - adjust the hot sauce amount to taste.
