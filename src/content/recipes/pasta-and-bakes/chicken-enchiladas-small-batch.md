## Instructions

1. Mix all of the spice ingredients in a small bowl.
2. Season the chicken with half the spice mix; set aside.
3. Heat oil in a skillet; cook the chicken 6-8 minutes per side until it reaches 165°F internal.
4. Remove the chicken and shred.
5. In the same pan, sauté the onion, jalapeño, and garlic for 5-6 minutes.
6. Add the tomato and the remaining spice mix; cook 3-4 minutes.
7. Combine the shredded chicken with the vegetable mixture.
8. Warm the tortillas; fill, roll, and place seam-side down in a 9x13 pan.
9. Cover with enchilada sauce and melting cheese.
10. Bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes until the cheese is melted and the edges are golden.

## Notes

* Makes 12 enchiladas, about 2 per serving.
* The reference doc does not quantify the sauce, so the amount here is an estimate.
* Nutrition is a rough estimate - verify and adjust.
