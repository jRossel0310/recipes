## Instructions

1. Heat the oil until it shimmers, then soften the onion.
2. Add the garlic, basil, red pepper flakes, salt, sugar, and butter.
3. Add half the tomatoes and scrape up the bottom of the pan. Turn the heat to low, then add the rest of the tomatoes and the water.
4. Simmer at least 10 minutes and up to an hour, adding water as needed.
5. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in well-salted water, drain, and toss with the sauce.

## Notes

* Leave out the butter (or use vegan butter) to make it vegan.
* The sauce freezes for up to three months.
* Nutrition is a rough estimate including the pasta - verify and adjust.
