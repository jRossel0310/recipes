## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Sauté the onion and garlic until soft, then add the celery and carrot.
2. Add the ground beef and brown it.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes, parsley, and basil. Simmer until thickened, about 30 minutes. Season with salt and pepper, then stir in the Pecorino.
4. Meanwhile, boil the pasta in well-salted water for about 2 minutes less than the package says.
5. Move the pasta into the sauce with tongs and toss to finish cooking.
