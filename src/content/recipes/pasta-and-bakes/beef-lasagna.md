## Instructions

**Beef ragu**

1. Brown the ground beef in a large pot, breaking it up as it cooks. Drain off the excess fat.
2. Add the marinara, season with salt, pepper, and Italian herbs, and simmer 20-30 minutes until thickened.

**Bechamel**

3. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook 3-5 minutes, until pale golden but not browned.
4. Slowly whisk in the warm milk. Simmer, whisking, until it coats a spoon but stays pourable, like heavy cream.
5. Season with the salt and nutmeg and keep warm.

**Assemble and bake**

6. Par-boil the lasagna sheets 4-5 minutes, until flexible but still firm. Skip this if using no-boil sheets.
7. Spread a thin layer of ragu across the bottom of a 9x13 pan.
8. Repeat this cycle three times: noodles in full coverage, a drizzle of bechamel, a layer of ragu, then a sprinkle of mozzarella.
9. Finish the top with noodles, a thick bechamel layer with no ragu, then a heavy layer of mozzarella and parmesan.
10. Cover tightly with foil and bake at 375°F for 40-45 minutes.
11. Uncover and bake another 15-20 minutes, until the cheese is browned and the edges are bubbling.
12. Rest 15-20 minutes before cutting, or it will come out sloppy.

## Notes

* One 9x13 pan, half of the co-op hotel pan. The bake time is shortened to match the smaller pan.
* No ricotta. The bechamel carries the creaminess.
