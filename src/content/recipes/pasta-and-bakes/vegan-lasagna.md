## Instructions

**Vegan ragu**

1. Saute the chopped onion in the olive oil until soft. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant.
2. Add the vegan mince and brown it well. Stir in the diced carrot and celery.
3. Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, and vegetable stock. Season with salt, pepper, and Italian herbs.
4. Simmer 20-30 minutes until thick. Do not rush this step - the tomatoes need the time to cook out and deepen.

**Vegan bechamel**

5. Melt the vegan butter, or heat the olive oil, in a saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook 3-5 minutes, until pale golden but not browned.
6. Slowly whisk in the warm plant milk. Simmer, whisking, until it coats a spoon but stays pourable, like heavy cream.
7. Season with the salt and nutmeg and keep warm.

**Assemble and bake**

8. Par-boil the lasagna sheets 4-5 minutes, until flexible but still firm. Skip this if using no-boil sheets.
9. Spread a thin layer of ragu across the bottom of a 9x13 pan.
10. Repeat this cycle three times: noodles in full coverage, a drizzle of bechamel, a layer of ragu, then a sprinkle of vegan mozzarella.
11. Finish the top with noodles, a thick bechamel layer with no ragu, then a heavy layer of vegan mozzarella.
12. Cover tightly with foil and bake at 375°F for 40-45 minutes.
13. Uncover and bake another 15-20 minutes, until the top is browned and the edges are bubbling.
14. Rest 15-20 minutes before cutting, or it will come out sloppy.

## Notes

* One 9x13 pan, half of the co-op hotel pan. The bake time is shortened to match the smaller pan.
* Check that the lasagna sheets are egg-free; many dry sheets are, but not all.
