## Instructions

1. Divide the beans, water, onion wedges, cumin, salt, oregano, and pepper among stockpots. Bring to a boil and skim off the foam.
2. Simmer on low for 1 hour, then check every 15 minutes until tender, up to 90 minutes more. Add water if the pots start to dry out.
3. Drain the beans, saving the cooking liquid.
4. Heat the oil in a tilt skillet or wide rondeaus over medium heat. Cook the diced onion and garlic about 5 minutes, until soft.
5. Add the beans and turn the heat to low. Mash with a potato masher, adding the cooking liquid in 4-cup amounts to reach the consistency you want (about 11-16 cups total). Use an immersion blender if you want them smoother.
6. Stir in the lime juice. Taste and add salt and lime juice. Expect up to another 3⅔ tbsp salt and ⅓ cup lime juice.

## Notes

* 22 cups dry beans plus 88 cups water needs three or four large stockpots. Each pot's water should cover the beans by 2-3 inches.
* The beans can be cooked a day ahead. Keep the cooking liquid for mashing.
* Canned version: 33 (14-oz) cans pinto beans, drained and rinsed. Skip steps 1-3, add the cumin, salt, and oregano with the beans, and mash with water or vegetable broth.
