## Instructions

1. Cook the basmati rice in the large rice cooker.
2. Sauté the onions in the oil until softened; add the garlic near the end.
3. Mix in the lemon juice, lemon zest, turmeric, salt, and pepper.
4. Fold the seasoning mixture into the cooked rice.
5. Taste and adjust the lemon, salt, and turmeric before service.

## Notes

* Makes roughly 70-75 cups of cooked rice, a reasonable side portion for ~170 people alongside pita, hummus, vegetables, chicken, and tofu.
