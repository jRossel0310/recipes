## Instructions

1. Drain the soaked beans, cover with fresh water, and simmer 45-60 minutes until fully tender. Drain, reserving some of the liquid. They will not soften any further once the sauce goes in.
2. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
3. Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat and cook the onion about 5 minutes, until translucent.
4. Add everything else except the beans and stir well, then stir in the beans.
5. Transfer to a 2-quart casserole dish or dutch oven with a few inches of headroom, cover, and bake 1 hour. Uncover and bake 30 minutes more.

## Notes

* Cook the beans past al dente. The acid and sugar in the sauce keep them from softening in the oven.
* For less sweet beans, cut the brown sugar and use tomato sauce instead of ketchup.
* Keeps 4-5 days in the fridge and freezes well.
