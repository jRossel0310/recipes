## Instructions

1. Dice the tomatoes and onion into small, even pieces.
2. Finely chop the cilantro.
3. Remove the jalapeno seeds and membranes for a milder pico, or leave some in for additional heat. Finely dice the jalapeno.
4. Combine the tomatoes, onion, cilantro, and jalapeno in a bowl.
5. Add the lime juice and salt.
6. Mix thoroughly and taste. Adjust the lime juice and salt as needed.
7. Refrigerate until serving. Stir before serving and drain excess liquid if necessary.
