## Instructions

1. Heat a large skillet until very hot. Add the neutral oil and the corn.
2. Let the corn sit undisturbed in places long enough to brown rather than stirring it constantly. Cook until hot throughout and lightly charred.
3. Lower the heat and stir in the butter, chili powder, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and the lime zest.
4. Finish with the lime juice and the cilantro right before serving.

## Notes

* For co-op scale, roast the corn on sheet pans in the convection oven instead of trying to saute huge batches in a skillet - you get the browning without steaming the corn.
* Use vegan butter to keep the dish vegan.
