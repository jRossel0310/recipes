## Instructions

1. Cut the cauliflower into medium florets. Cut the carrots into pieces slightly smaller than the cauliflower so both finish at roughly the same time.
2. Toss with the olive oil, oregano, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
3. Spread across a sheet pan without overcrowding.
4. Roast at 425°F until browned and tender, 25-35 minutes, tossing halfway through.
5. Finish with the fresh lemon juice before serving.
