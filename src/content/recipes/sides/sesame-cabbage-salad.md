## Instructions

1. Very thinly slice the cabbage, shred the carrots, and slice the green onions into a large bowl.
2. Whisk together the rice vinegar, soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, neutral oil, sugar, ginger, and garlic.
3. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss to coat.
4. Let the salad sit 15-30 minutes before serving so it softens slightly and takes on the dressing.
5. Toss with the sesame seeds just before serving.

## Notes

* Keep this one acidic and crisp - it is meant to cut through the fried chicken and BBQ sauce rather than be another heavy side. Add a splash more rice vinegar if it tastes flat.
* Use tamari instead of soy sauce to keep the salad gluten-free.
* Dress no more than about an hour ahead at scale, or the cabbage weeps and goes limp.
