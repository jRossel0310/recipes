## Instructions

**Prepare the green beans:**

1. Trim and halve all the green beans.
2. Bring large stockpots of salted water to a boil.
3. Blanch the green beans in manageable batches until just tender.
4. Transfer immediately to ice water to stop the cooking.
5. Drain and dry thoroughly.
6. Do all of this before the onions so the green beans have time to dry.

**Prepare the crispy onions:**

7. Thinly slice the onions with a mandoline.
8. Coat evenly with rice flour.
9. Fry in batches in oil heated to about 375°F.
10. Cook until golden and crispy.
11. Drain and season.
12. Store appropriately until casserole assembly.

**Prepare the mushroom sauce:**

13. Melt the butter in large, heavy-bottomed stockpots, working in manageable batches.
14. Add the mushrooms, salt, and pepper.
15. Cook until the mushrooms release their moisture and begin browning.
16. Add the garlic and nutmeg and stir until fragrant.
17. Add the flour and cook briefly while stirring.
18. Add the sherry vinegar.
19. Gradually add the chicken broth, stirring continuously.
20. Add the half-and-half.
21. Simmer until the sauce thickens substantially.

**Assemble and bake:**

22. Combine the blanched green beans with the mushroom sauce.
23. Stir in about half of the crispy onions.
24. Divide among rice pots to go in the oven.
25. Refrigerate the assembled casserole if preparing ahead.
26. Top with the remaining crispy onions near baking time.
27. Bake at 350°F until bubbling throughout and golden.
28. Make sure cold, deep pans heat all the way through before serving.

## Notes

* Alton Brown's recipe, scaled 29×.
* Adjust blanching salt to the amount of water and number of batches rather than multiplying the original water-salting quantity.
* Contains dairy and chicken broth - not vegan or vegetarian.
