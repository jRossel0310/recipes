## Instructions

1. Toss the cubed potatoes with the oil.
2. Add all the seasonings and mix until evenly coated.
3. Spread in a single layer on sheet trays without overcrowding, and roast at 425°F (220°C) for 35-45 minutes depending on cube size, flipping halfway through.
4. If using Parmesan, sprinkle it on during the last 5 minutes or immediately after they come out of the oven.
5. Finish with a little chopped parsley or chives if you have them.

## Notes

* Scaled from the home Seasoned Roasted Potatoes recipe at 750 g potatoes per batch of seasoning, about 18 batches for 30 lb.
* 30 lb across 150 people is about 90 g of potatoes each, roughly half the home recipe's portion. Nutrition is adjusted to match and is a rough estimate.
