## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease the baking pans.
2. Tear or cube the bread and make sure it is dry.
3. Sauté the leeks, celery, garlic, salt, and pepper in butter for about 5 minutes.
4. Combine the bread with the herbs and sautéed vegetables.
5. Add the beaten eggs.
6. Add broth gradually until the mixture reaches the desired moisture level.
7. Transfer to the greased baking pans.
8. Pour the melted butter over the top.
9. Cover and bake for about 30 minutes.
10. Uncover and bake another 5-10 minutes.
11. Check that the center reaches 165°F.

## Notes

* Contains eggs, so it must reach 165°F in the center.
* Vegetarian only if made with vegetable broth - the plan doesn't specify which broth.
* Nutrition is a rough estimate - verify and adjust.
