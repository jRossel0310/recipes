## Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes and cut into relatively even pieces.
2. Divide the potatoes among large stockpots and cover with about 1 inch of water.
3. Add the salt and bring to a gentle boil.
4. Cook about 25-30 minutes, until very tender.
5. Sauté the garlic while the potatoes cook.
6. Drain the potatoes thoroughly.
7. Return the potatoes to the hot pots briefly to evaporate excess moisture.
8. Mash until fluffy.
9. Mix in the vegan butter, garlic, salt, and pepper.
10. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
11. Garnish with chives if using.
12. Hold at a safe serving temperature (135°F or above) until dinner.
