## Instructions

1. Combine the cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion in a large bowl.
2. Whisk together the olive oil, vinegar, salt, pepper, and sugar (if using) in a small bowl.
3. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss to coat.
4. Sprinkle with fresh herbs if using. Let sit at least 10 minutes before serving so the flavors meld.
5. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

## Notes

* Best made close to serving time - cucumbers release water as they sit and can turn the salad watery.
* If making further ahead, prep the vegetables and dressing separately and toss just before serving.
