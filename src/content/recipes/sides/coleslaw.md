## Instructions

1. Shred the cabbage and carrot into a large bowl.
2. Whisk together the mayonnaise, sugar, apple cider vinegar, lemon juice, salt, and black pepper until smooth.
3. Pour the dressing over the slaw and toss to coat evenly.
4. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes before serving so the flavors combine.
