## Instructions

1. Chop the cucumbers and tomatoes into bite-size pieces. Thinly slice or dice the red onion and chop the parsley.
2. Combine the vegetables shortly before dinner.
3. Whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper.
4. Toss the salad with the dressing shortly before service.
5. Put the feta out separately so the base salad stays vegan.

## Notes

* Intentionally scaled as a small fresh side, because people are mainly eating chicken or tofu, rice, roasted vegetables, pita, hummus, and tzatziki.
* Nutrition is for the base salad without feta.
