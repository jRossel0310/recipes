## Instructions

1. Cook the basmati rice with the water in a rice cooker.
2. Sauté the onion in the oil until softened; add the garlic near the end.
3. Mix in the lemon juice, lemon zest, turmeric, salt, and pepper.
4. Fold the seasoning mixture into the cooked rice.
5. Taste and adjust the lemon, salt, and turmeric before serving.
