## Instructions

1. **Boiled:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the corn and cook 5-7 minutes until bright yellow and tender. Drain.
2. **Grilled (alternative):** Preheat a grill to medium-high. Grill the corn directly on the grates, turning occasionally, 10-12 minutes until charred in spots and tender.
3. Brush the hot corn all over with melted butter and season with salt and pepper to taste.
4. Serve immediately.

## Notes

* For a smokier flavor, grill the corn in the husk (soak 10 minutes first, then peel back the husk before grilling).
