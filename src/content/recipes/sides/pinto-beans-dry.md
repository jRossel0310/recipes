## Instructions

1. Drain the soaked beans, cover with fresh water by 2 inches, and simmer 60-90 minutes until tender. Salt in the last 15 minutes. Drain, reserving the cooking liquid.
2. Heat a pan over medium heat with a little oil. Sauté the onion 2-3 minutes until softened.
3. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
4. Add the beans, cumin, chili powder, cinnamon, black pepper, and enough reserved liquid to loosen.
5. Simmer 8-12 minutes, stirring occasionally. Mash a few spoonfuls against the pan if you want it thicker.
6. Stir in the cilantro near the end, then taste for salt.

## Notes

* 1 cup dry beans ≈ 2 cans drained. Soak and simmer can both be done the day before.
* Keep the cinnamon light; it adds warmth without reading as cinnamon.
