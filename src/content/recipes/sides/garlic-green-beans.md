## Instructions

1. Boil the beans in salted water for 4 minutes. Drain and rinse under cold water.
2. Heat the oil over medium-high heat. Add the beans and garlic and toss for about 2 minutes, until the garlic is golden.
3. Season with salt and pepper and serve warm.

## Notes

* Make ahead: blanch, cool, dry, and refrigerate the beans, then sauté with the garlic just before serving.
* Trim only the stem tip and keep the beans whole. Mince the garlic with a knife rather than a press.
* Serves 4 as a small side (2 as a large side). Nutrition is the source's per-serving figure halved to match 4 servings.
