## Instructions

1. Cut the cauliflower into medium florets. Cut the carrots into pieces slightly smaller than the cauliflower so both finish at roughly the same time.
2. Toss with the olive oil, oregano, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
3. Spread across sheet pans without overcrowding.
4. Roast at 425°F until browned and tender, tossing and rotating as needed.
5. Finish with the fresh lemon juice before service.

## Notes

* Roughly 40-45 lb of raw vegetables in total, depending on the size of the cauliflower.
