## Instructions

1. Wash and thoroughly dry all the vegetables.
2. Chop the romaine into bite-sized pieces.
3. Dice the cucumber, tomatoes, and bell pepper. Thinly slice or dice the radishes and red onion, and chop the cilantro.
4. Combine all the vegetables in a large bowl.
5. For the vinaigrette, whisk together the lime juice, oil, agave or sugar, cumin, salt, and black pepper.
6. Taste and adjust the salt, sweetness, or lime juice as needed.
7. Dress the salad and toss to coat just before serving.
