## Instructions

1. Wash and thoroughly dry all vegetables.
2. Chop the romaine into bite-sized pieces.
3. Dice the cucumbers, tomatoes, and bell peppers.
4. Thinly slice or dice the radishes.
5. Finely dice or thinly slice the red onion.
6. Chop the cilantro.
7. Combine all vegetables in large serving containers.
8. For the vinaigrette, combine the lime juice, oil, agave or sugar, cumin, salt, and black pepper.
9. Whisk thoroughly until well combined.
10. Taste and adjust the salt, sweetness, or lime juice as needed.
11. Dress the salad and toss to coat just before service.
