## Instructions

1. Stir the oil and rice in a large pot on the stove for about 6 minutes, until light gold.
2. Add the onions and garlic. Cook 2 minutes.
3. Add the tomato sauce and cook 3 minutes.
4. Add the broth and salt to taste. Bring to a boil.
5. Once boiling, cover the pot and turn the heat to low. Cook 20 minutes.
6. Let rest 10 minutes, then fluff.

## Notes

* One recipe fills one large pot. For a bigger crowd, make it again in a second pot rather than doubling it in one.
* Nutrition is a rough estimate - verify and adjust.
