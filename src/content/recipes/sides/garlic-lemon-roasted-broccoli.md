## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F conventional or 400°F convection.
2. Toss the broccoli florets with the olive oil, minced garlic, salt, garlic powder, and black pepper - everything except the lemon.
3. Spread the broccoli in a single layer on a sheet pan. Do not crowd the pan.
4. Roast about 15-20 minutes, tossing once, until the edges are browned. Pull it while the florets still have bite - do not roast until soft and gray.
5. Squeeze the lemon juice over the broccoli right before serving. Add red pepper flakes if using.

## Notes

* Crowding the pan steams the broccoli instead of roasting it. At scale, use more pans rather than deeper piles.
* Add the lemon juice at the last moment - it dulls the color if it sits.
