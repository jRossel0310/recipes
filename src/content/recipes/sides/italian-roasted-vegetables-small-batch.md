## Instructions

1. Cut the vegetables to similar cooking size rather than identical physical size: carrots smaller, broccoli into medium florets, bell pepper into large strips or chunks, onion into thick wedges, and zucchini into relatively large pieces. Large zucchini pieces keep them from going mushy.
2. Toss the vegetables with the olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning.
3. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan, or two if they are crowded.
4. Roast at 400°F for 20-30 minutes, stirring halfway through.
