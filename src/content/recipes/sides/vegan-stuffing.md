## Instructions

1. Cube the bread.
2. Dry overnight, or bake at 275°F convection for about 20-25 minutes.
3. Remove the sausage casings if necessary.
4. Crumble and brown the sausage in batches.
5. Sauté the onions, celery, and carrots in olive oil for about 10-12 minutes.
6. Add the garlic, herbs, and any optional ingredients.
7. Combine the dried bread, vegetables, and sausage.
8. Gradually mix in the vegetable broth until moistened but not soggy.
9. Transfer to greased baking pans.
10. Bake uncovered at 325°F convection for about 35-45 minutes.
11. Add about 10-15 minutes if baking from cold, and check that the center is heated through.
