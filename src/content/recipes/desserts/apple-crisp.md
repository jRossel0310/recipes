## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a deep-dish pie plate or large baking dish with butter or cooking spray.
2. Toss the sliced apples with the sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla until evenly coated. Pour into the prepared dish.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, oats, brown sugar, salt, and cinnamon. Stir in the melted butter until crumbly. Sprinkle evenly over the apples.
4. Bake 45-60 minutes, until the fruit is soft and the topping is golden brown. Cool slightly before serving.

## Notes

* "Servings" here counts pans: one recipe makes one deep-dish pie plate. The source doesn't give a per-person serving count.
* Slice the apples evenly (about 1/2 inch at the widest part) so they bake at the same rate. Big uniform chunks work too.
* If the topping browns before the apples are soft, cover the pan loosely with foil and keep baking.
* Chopped pecans or walnuts are a good addition to the topping.
* Serve warm with vanilla ice cream, or whipped cream and caramel sauce.
