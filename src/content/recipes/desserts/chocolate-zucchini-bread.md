## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan with nonstick cooking spray and set aside.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa, baking soda, and sea salt. Set aside.
3. In a large bowl, stir the eggs, melted butter, oil, vanilla, and brown sugar until smooth. A few small brown sugar clumps are fine.
4. Stir the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients without overmixing. Stir in the shredded zucchini until just combined, then stir in 3/4 cup of the chocolate chips.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and sprinkle the remaining 1/4 cup of chocolate chips over the top.
6. Bake 50-60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out mostly clean. Melted chocolate on the toothpick is fine; gooey batter is not.
7. Set the pan on a wire rack and let the bread cool in the pan for 15 minutes. Run a knife around the edges, carefully remove the bread from the pan, and let it cool on the rack until slightly warm. Slice and serve.

## Notes

* If using coconut oil, make sure it is melted and slightly cooled.
* Keeps on the counter, wrapped in plastic wrap, for up to 4 days.
* To freeze, cool completely, wrap in plastic wrap and aluminum foil, and freeze for up to 1 month. Defrost before slicing.
* Gram weights are estimated from the cup measures.
