## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 9x13-inch cake pan.
2. Combine the soy milk and apple cider vinegar and set aside.
3. In a large bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
4. Add the milk mixture, oil, applesauce, and vanilla to the dry ingredients and stir until combined.
5. Pour half the batter into the prepared pan.
6. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon for the streusel.
7. Sprinkle half the streusel over the batter, then add the remaining batter.
8. Top with the remaining streusel and drizzle with the melted vegan butter.
9. Bake 40-45 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

## Notes

* Halve the recipe for an 8x8-inch pan (about 8 servings).
* For oil-free, substitute applesauce for the oil and omit the butter drizzle.
* For whole grain, use white whole wheat or whole wheat pastry flour.
* For gluten-free, use a quality gluten-free all-purpose flour blend.
