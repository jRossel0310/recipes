## Instructions

1. In a small saucepan, combine the pectin, water, and ¼ cup sugar.
2. In a separate, larger saucepan, combine the remaining ½ cup sugar, salt, vanilla bean paste, heavy cream, and half and half. Whisk constantly while bringing the mixture up to about 185°F.
3. Once the base reaches 185°F, remove it from the heat.
4. Bring the pectin mixture to a boil over medium heat and let it boil for 1 minute.
5. Whisk the boiled pectin mixture into the hot base until fully combined.
6. Cool quickly in an ice bath.
7. Either churn immediately, or refrigerate and churn later.
