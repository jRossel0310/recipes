## Instructions

1. Optionally roast the turkey necks and giblets at 425°F for 25-30 minutes.
2. Break up any available roasted carcass.
3. Combine the turkey parts, vegetables, and herbs in large stockpots.
4. Cover with cold water.
5. Bring to a gentle simmer.
6. Simmer about 4-6 hours.
7. Add water as necessary.
8. Strain and chill rapidly.
9. Refrigerate overnight.
10. Measure the finished stock and supplement with prepared stock if needed.

## Notes

* Target: at least 4-5 quarts of strained stock.
