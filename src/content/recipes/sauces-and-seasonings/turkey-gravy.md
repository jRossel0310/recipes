## Instructions

1. Collect the drippings from the roasted turkeys.
2. Separate the rendered fat from the liquid drippings.
3. Heat the fat in a large, heavy-bottomed pot.
4. Whisk in the flour.
5. Cook until the roux turns lightly golden.
6. Gradually whisk in the liquid drippings.
7. Gradually add the turkey stock while whisking continuously.
8. Simmer until smooth and thickened, about 5-10 minutes or as needed.
9. Adjust the consistency with additional stock.
10. Season with salt and pepper.
11. Keep hot until serving.

## Notes

* Target: about 5-6 quarts of finished gravy, adjusted with additional stock as needed.
* Nutrition is a rough estimate (about 2 tbsp per serving) - verify and adjust.
