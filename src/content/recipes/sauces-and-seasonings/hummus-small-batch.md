## Instructions

1. For the smoothest hummus, simmer the drained chickpeas in water for 15-20 minutes until very soft, then drain. Skip this if you are short on time.
2. Whip the tahini and lemon juice in a food processor first.
3. Add the olive oil, garlic, cumin, and salt.
4. Add the chickpeas and process until smooth.
5. Add cold water as needed to reach a creamy consistency.
6. Taste and adjust the lemon and salt.
7. Finish with olive oil and paprika, sumac, or za'atar.

## Notes

* Makes about 1¾ cups. This is the single batch the co-op version is multiplied from.
