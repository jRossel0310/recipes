## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef in a large pot, breaking it up as it cooks.
2. Drain off the excess fat.
3. Add the red sauce and stir to combine.
4. Season with salt, pepper, and Italian herbs.
5. Simmer 20-30 minutes until thickened. Do not rush it - the sauce should reduce and deepen rather than stay loose.

## Notes

* One hotel pan's worth. 20 servings is one pan, so 120 servings is 6 pans, 20 lb of beef and 5 gallons of red sauce.
