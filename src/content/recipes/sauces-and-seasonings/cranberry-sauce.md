## Instructions

1. Rinse the cranberries thoroughly.
2. Combine the sugar and water in a large pot.
3. Bring to a boil.
4. Add the cranberries.
5. Simmer about 10 minutes, until the cranberries burst.
6. Add any optional flavorings.
7. Taste and adjust the sweetness.
8. Cool and refrigerate until serving.
