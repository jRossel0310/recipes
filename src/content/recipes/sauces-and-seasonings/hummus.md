## Instructions

1. Drain the soaked chickpeas and cook until very soft. Reserve some of the cooking liquid.
2. Work in manageable food-processor batches rather than attempting the whole quantity at once.
3. Whip the tahini and lemon juice first.
4. Add the olive oil, garlic, cumin, and salt.
5. Add the cooked chickpeas and process until smooth.
6. Add cold water or reserved chickpea liquid as needed to reach a creamy consistency.
7. Combine the individual batches in a large container and adjust the lemon and salt across the entire batch.
8. Finish with olive oil and paprika, sumac, or za'atar.

## Notes

* Built around 5 L of dry chickpeas, which yields roughly 9-10 kg cooked depending on the chickpeas and the cooking.
* The original recipe uses 250 g of cooked chickpeas per batch, so this works out to roughly 36-40 batches.
