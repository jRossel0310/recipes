## Instructions

1. Grate the cucumber and squeeze out the excess liquid.
2. Combine the cucumber with the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, salt, dill, and mint.
3. Taste and adjust the salt and lemon.
4. Refrigerate until service.

## Notes

* Makes about 1½ gallons of finished tzatziki.
