## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Whisk in the flour and cook 5-7 minutes, until pale golden but not browned.
3. Slowly whisk in the warm milk.
4. Simmer, whisking, until thick and smooth. It should coat a spoon but stay pourable, like heavy cream - not pasty.
5. Season with the salt and nutmeg.
6. Keep warm for assembly.

## Notes

* This is one hotel pan's worth, about 1 to 1.25 quarts. 20 servings is one pan.
