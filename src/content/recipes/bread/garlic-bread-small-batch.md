## Instructions

1. Mix the softened or melted butter with the minced garlic, chopped parsley, a pinch of salt, and the Italian seasoning if using.
2. Slice the loaf lengthwise.
3. Coat the cut surfaces generously with the garlic butter and top with the parmesan.
4. Arrange cut-side up on a sheet pan.
5. Bake at 375°F for 8-12 minutes, until the butter has melted into the bread, the edges are lightly crisp, and the parmesan is beginning to brown.
