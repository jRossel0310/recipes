## Instructions

**Prepare the dough:**

1. Combine the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, warm milk, softened butter, and eggs in the bowl of a stand mixer.
2. Attach the dough hook and mix on the lowest speed until the flour is incorporated.
3. Increase the speed to medium and mix for 2 minutes.
4. Gradually add more flour in about ½-cup increments.
5. Keep mixing until a soft, slightly sticky ball forms and pulls away from the bowl.

**First rise:**

6. Transfer the dough to a lightly greased bowl.
7. Cover with a towel or plastic wrap.
8. Let rise at room temperature for about 30 minutes.

**Shape the rolls:**

9. Gently punch down the dough.
10. Divide into portions of about 2 oz (57 g).
11. Shape each portion into a smooth roll.
12. Place the rolls in lightly greased quarter-sheet pans or 9×13-inch baking dishes.

**Second rise:**

13. Cover the shaped rolls.
14. Let rise about 30 minutes at room temperature.

**Bake:**

15. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
16. Bake for about 12-15 minutes.
17. Tent with foil if browning too quickly.
18. Remove when golden brown and cooked through.
19. Brush the hot rolls with melted butter.

## Notes

* Make ahead: bake the rolls before Thanksgiving Day, cool completely, then refrigerate or freeze depending on how far ahead. Reheat near dinner service, using oven space after the other major dishes finish.
* Nine batches of a 24-roll recipe.
